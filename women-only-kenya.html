Women-Only Kenya Highlights

8 days Mara · Amboseli · Nairobi Hosted group of 8–12 from $8,200 / person

Home / Kenya / Women-Only Kenya Highlights

Our flagship sisterhood departure. Eight days through Kenya's icons — the Maasai Mara, Amboseli and Nairobi — in a small hosted group of 8 to 12 women, led by a female host, in vetted, women-friendly camps. Arrive solo; leave with a circle.

Everything is handled, so all you do is show up and feel. Safety, warmth and real connection are the heart of every day.

Day by day

Your 8-day journey

Day 1

Arrive & gather in Nairobi

Met on arrival and brought together for a warm welcome dinner — meet your host and the women you'll share the journey with.

Stay: Nairobi hotel · Meals: Dinner

Days 2–4

Maasai Mara

Fly to the Mara for three days of big cats and plains game, an optional balloon dawn, sundowners on the savanna, and an afternoon with Maasai women.

Stay: Women-friendly tented camp · Meals: All

Days 5–6

Amboseli & Kilimanjaro

Fly to Amboseli for elephants beneath Kilimanjaro, golden-hour drives, and slow evenings together under big skies.

Stay: Amboseli lodge · Meals: All

Day 7

Nairobi & giving back

Return to Nairobi for the Sheldrick elephant orphanage and a visit to a women-led community project we support, then a celebration dinner.

Stay: Nairobi hotel · Meals: Breakfast & dinner

Day 8

Farewell

Goodbyes (and new friendships) before your onward flight — or extend to Zanzibar with your new circle.

Meals: Breakfast

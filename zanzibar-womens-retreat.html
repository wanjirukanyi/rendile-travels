Zanzibar · Itinerary

Zanzibar Women's Retreat

7 days Stone Town · North Coast Women-only from $6,200 / person
Enquire about this trip
Home / Tanzania / Zanzibar Women's Retreat

A week of sun, spice and sisterhood on the Indian Ocean. Daily beachfront yoga, the spice-scented lanes of Stone Town, a traditional dhow under sail and long barefoot days — shared with a small circle of like-minded women and a Rendile hostess throughout.

Gentle and restorative rather than rushed: come to slow down, reconnect and breathe. Solo travellers, mother-and-daughter pairs and groups of friends are all warmly welcome.

Day by day

Your 7-day journey

Day 1

Karibu Zanzibar

Met at the airport and taken into historic Stone Town. Settle into a boutique hotel, then gather for a welcome dinner of Swahili mezze as the call to prayer drifts over the rooftops.

Stay: Stone Town boutique hotel · Meals: Dinner

Day 2

Stone Town & the spice route

A guided walk through the UNESCO old town — carved doors, the market and the Old Fort. In the afternoon, a spice-farm visit and a hands-on Swahili cooking class.

Stay: Stone Town boutique hotel · Meals: Breakfast, lunch

Day 3

North to the coast

Drive to the powder-white beaches of the north. Settle into your beachfront retreat, then ease into the week with a sunset yoga session on the sand.

Stay: Beachfront resort · Meals: All

Day 4

Yoga, ocean & rest

Morning yoga and meditation, then the day is yours — swim, read, or snorkel the reef. An optional spa treatment and a moonlit beach dinner close the day.

Stay: Beachfront resort · Meals: All

Day 5

Dhow sailing & a sandbank picnic

Board a traditional dhow for a day on the turquoise water — snorkelling, a barefoot sandbank picnic and a slow sail home into the sunset.

Stay: Beachfront resort · Meals: All

Day 6

Jozani forest & a closing circle

An optional morning at Jozani to meet the rare red colobus monkeys, then a restorative afternoon — final yoga, journaling and a celebratory farewell feast.

Stay: Beachfront resort · Meals: All

Day 7

Farewell to the island

A last sunrise stretch on the beach, then transfer to the airport for your onward flight — rested, restored and already planning the next.

Meals: Breakfast

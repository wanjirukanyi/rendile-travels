Why Rwanda

Land of a thousand hills.

Rwanda is East Africa's gentlest surprise — a small, green, remarkably safe country of mist-wrapped volcanoes and a thousand rolling hills. In the Volcanoes National Park you can sit metres from a family of mountain gorillas; in Nyungwe you walk a canopy bridge above ancient rainforest; and in Akagera the savanna and its Big Five have been brought roaring back.

For women travelling with us, Rwanda is calm, clean and deeply welcoming: superb small lodges, warm hospitality, expert trackers, female guides where possible, and a Rendile team alongside you every step of the way.

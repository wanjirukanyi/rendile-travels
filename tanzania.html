Why Tanzania

Where the wild runs widest.

Tanzania is safari at its grandest — the Serengeti's endless plains, the Ngorongoro Crater's natural amphitheatre of wildlife, and over a million wildebeest thundering across the Mara River. Beyond the savanna, snow-capped Kilimanjaro rises over the north and the spice island of Zanzibar waits on the warm Indian Ocean.

For women travelling with us, Tanzania is wonderfully rewarding and easy: superb camps, genuine hospitality, female guides where possible, and a Rendile team on the ground every step of the way.

Amboseli: Elephants & Kilimanjaro

Great herds beneath Africa's highest peak.

Home / Kenya / Amboseli

Few sights in Africa are as photographed — or as moving — as a herd of elephants crossing Amboseli's plains with the snows of Mount Kilimanjaro behind them. Compact, easy and reliably spectacular, Amboseli is one of Kenya's most rewarding parks for women travellers.

Why visit Amboseli?

Amboseli National Park sits in southern Kenya on the Tanzanian border, beneath Kilimanjaro. Its open, swampy plains make wildlife easy to spot, and the light at dawn and dusk — with the mountain glowing — is unforgettable. It pairs beautifully with the Maasai Mara for a classic Kenya safari.

The elephants of Amboseli

Amboseli is home to some of Africa's largest and most studied elephants, including grand old "tuskers." Watching multi-generational families move through the marshes — matriarchs, aunties and tiny calves — is the park's signature experience, and a quietly powerful one.

Views of Mount Kilimanjaro

On clear mornings the mountain dominates the horizon. The skies are clearest at sunrise and just before sunset, so early and late game drives reward you with both wildlife and that iconic backdrop.

Other wildlife

  • Lion, cheetah and spotted hyena
  • Buffalo, giraffe, zebra and wildebeest
  • Hippo and abundant water birds in the swamps
  • Over 400 bird species, including flamingos after rains

Best time to visit Amboseli

The dry seasons — June to October and January to February — offer the clearest Kilimanjaro views and the best game viewing, as animals gather at the swamps. The greener low season (April–May) is quieter and lovely for birds. See our best time to visit Kenya guide for the full picture.

Where to stay

We hand-pick small, women-friendly lodges and tented camps with Kilimanjaro views and warm service — many with female guides. For privacy, camps in the surrounding Maasai conservancies add walking safaris and night drives.

How to get there

Amboseli is a short flight from Nairobi's Wilson Airport, or around a 4-hour road transfer. We arrange every leg, door to door, and combine it seamlessly with the Mara, Tsavo or the coast.

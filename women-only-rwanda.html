Rwanda · Itinerary

Women-Only Rwanda Retreat

6 days Volcanoes · Lake Kivu Hosted group of 8–12 from $7,800 / person
Enquire about this trip
Home / Rwanda / Women-Only Rwanda Retreat

Our sisterhood departure to Rwanda blends one of the world's most moving wildlife encounters with genuine rest — a morning with the mountain gorillas, then wellness, reflection and connection by the calm shores of Lake Kivu, in an intimate group of 8–12 women led by a female host.

Vetted lodges, gentle pacing and time with the women's cooperatives we support. Come solo and leave with friends.

Day by day

Your 6-day journey

Day 1

Arrive & gather in Kigali

Met at the airport and welcomed with a visit to the women-led Nyamirambo neighbourhood, then a welcome dinner with your host in the capital.

Stay: Kigali boutique hotel · Meals: Dinner

Day 2

To the Volcanoes

Drive north through terraced hills to the foot of the Virungas. An afternoon of gentle yoga and a briefing for the trek to come.

Stay: Volcanoes-area lodge · Meals: All

Day 3

Gorilla trekking

The morning you came for — into the forest with trackers ahead for a magical, regulated hour with a mountain gorilla family. The afternoon is yours to rest and reflect together.

Stay: Volcanoes-area lodge · Meals: All

Day 4

To Lake Kivu

A scenic descent to the lake. Sunset yoga on the shore and a quiet first evening by the water.

Stay: Lake Kivu lodge · Meals: All

Day 5

Kivu wellness & women's cooperative

Morning yoga, a coffee-farm visit and time with a women's cooperative — weaving, stories and shared chai — then a free afternoon to swim, paddle or simply be.

Stay: Lake Kivu lodge · Meals: All

Day 6

Return to Kigali & farewell

A final sunrise stretch by the lake, then a scenic drive back to Kigali for your onward flight — rested, restored and among new friends.

Meals: Breakfast

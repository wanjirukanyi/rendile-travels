Kigali, Culture & Kivu

6 days Kigali · Volcanoes foothills · Lake Kivu Mothers & children from $5,600 / person
Enquire about this trip
Home / Rwanda / Kigali, Culture & Kivu

A gentle, culture-rich Rwanda for families — the creative buzz of Kigali, playful golden monkeys in the Virunga foothills, and lakeside days swimming and sailing on Lake Kivu. Short drives, warm welcomes and plenty for curious young minds.

Rwanda is clean, safe and exceptionally welcoming, which makes it a wonderful introduction to Africa for children. (Gorilla trekking has a minimum age of 15, so this journey keeps younger travellers happily occupied elsewhere.)

Day by day

Your 6-day journey

Day 1

Arrive in Kigali

Met at the airport and taken to a welcoming hotel with gardens and a pool. An easy afternoon to settle in after the flight.

Stay: Kigali hotel · Meals: Dinner

Day 2

Creative Kigali

A hands-on day in the capital — the colourful Inema arts centre, a visit to the women-led Nyamirambo neighbourhood for cooking and crafts, and the lively Kimironko market.

Stay: Kigali hotel · Meals: Breakfast, lunch

Day 3

North to the Virunga foothills

Drive through terraced hills to the foot of the volcanoes, with a stop at the equator and a community cultural village where children can drum, dance and learn.

Stay: Volcanoes-area lodge · Meals: All

Day 4

Golden monkeys & caves

Track the playful golden monkeys through the bamboo — a gentler, family-friendly forest walk — then explore the lava-tube Musanze caves.

Stay: Volcanoes-area lodge · Meals: All

Day 5

To Lake Kivu

A short, scenic drive to the lake for swimming, kayaking and a boat to the islands, with sundowners over the water.

Stay: Lake Kivu lodge · Meals: All

Day 6

Return & farewell

A relaxed morning by the lake, then back to Kigali for your onward flight — full hearts and full memory cards.

Meals: Breakfast

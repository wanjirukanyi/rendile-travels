Swahili Coast Escape

3 days · 2 nights Swahili Coast · Beach Resident rates available from $890 / person

Home / Quick escapes / Swahili Coast Escape

Trade the city for the Indian Ocean for a long weekend. Fly to the Swahili coast, swap your shoes for the sand, and let two nights of dhow sails, warm water and spice-scented feasts slow everything right down.

Designed for residents and short-stay travellers, with easy fly-in logistics and resident rates. We tailor it to Diani, Lamu or Watamu to suit your taste.

Day by day

Your weekend

Fri

Fly to the coast

A short flight to the coast, then straight to your beachfront retreat. Toes in the sand, a swim, and a sunset cocktail to begin.

Stay: Beachfront hotel · Meals: Dinner

Sat

Ocean day

A traditional dhow cruise with snorkelling over the reef and a fresh-seafood lunch on the water, then a free afternoon to laze, read or take a spa treatment.

Stay: Beachfront hotel · Meals: All

Sun

Morning & fly home

A last slow morning by the sea — a swim, a long breakfast — then transfer to the airstrip for your flight home.

Meals: Breakfast

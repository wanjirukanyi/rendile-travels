Uganda · Itinerary

Classic Uganda Safari

10 days Murchison · Kibale · Queen Elizabeth · Bwindi Private or small-group from $7,900 / person
Enquire about this trip
Home / Uganda / Classic Uganda Safari

The grand loop of the "pearl of Africa" — the thundering Nile at Murchison Falls, chimpanzees in Kibale, the lions and elephants of Queen Elizabeth, and an unforgettable hour with the mountain gorillas of Bwindi.

It's Uganda's wild heart in full, paced so the long drives become part of the adventure — tea estates, equator crossings and terraced green hills all the way.

Day by day

Your 10-day journey

Day 1

Arrive in Entebbe

Met at the airport and taken to a restful lake-shore hotel to settle in and meet your guide.

Stay: Entebbe lake hotel · Meals: Dinner

Day 2

To Murchison Falls

Drive north to Murchison Falls National Park, pausing at the Ziwa rhino sanctuary for a walking encounter on the way.

Stay: Murchison riverside lodge · Meals: All

Day 3

Murchison Falls

A morning game drive for elephant, giraffe and lion, then an afternoon boat cruise up the Nile to the foot of the falls, where the river forces through a seven-metre gorge.

Stay: Murchison riverside lodge · Meals: All

Day 4

South to Kibale

A scenic drive to the Kibale forest, with the Rwenzori "Mountains of the Moon" rising to the west.

Stay: Kibale forest lodge · Meals: All

Day 5

Chimpanzee trekking

A morning among the chimpanzees of Kibale, then a Bigodi wetland walk rich in monkeys and birds.

Stay: Kibale forest lodge · Meals: All

Day 6

Queen Elizabeth National Park

Into Queen Elizabeth for an afternoon cruise on the Kazinga Channel — elephant, buffalo, hippo and a wall of birds along the banks.

Stay: Queen Elizabeth lodge · Meals: All

Day 7

Ishasha tree-climbing lions

A game drive through the remote Ishasha sector, famous for its tree-climbing lions draped in the fig trees, before continuing toward gorilla country.

Stay: Bwindi forest lodge · Meals: All

Day 8

Gorilla trekking in Bwindi

The journey's crescendo — a hike into the Bwindi Impenetrable Forest to spend a regulated hour with a mountain gorilla family.

Stay: Bwindi forest lodge · Meals: All

Day 9

Lake Bunyonyi

A short drive to the terraced shores of Lake Bunyonyi — a restful day of canoes, birdsong and reflection after the forest.

Stay: Lake Bunyonyi lodge · Meals: All

Day 10

Fly to Entebbe & depart

A light-aircraft flight back to Entebbe for your onward connection.

Meals: Breakfast

Gorillas in a Weekend

3 days · 2 nights Volcanoes NP · Fly-in Permit included from $2,400 / person
Enquire about this escape
Home / Quick escapes / Gorillas in a Weekend

Already in the region? You're closer to the mountain gorillas than you think. Fly to Kigali, drive into the misty Volcanoes National Park, spend a soul-stirring hour with a gorilla family, and be home by Sunday — a bucket-list moment in a single weekend.

Designed for residents and short-stay travellers, with the gorilla permit, lodge and transfers all arranged. Trekking is for ages 15 and up; permits are limited, so we book early.

Day by day

Your weekend

Fri

Fly to Kigali, drive to the volcanoes

Arrive in Kigali and transfer about two and a half hours through the terraced hills to a lodge at the foot of the Virungas. Settle in, with a briefing for the trek ahead.

Stay: Volcanoes-area lodge · Meals: Dinner

Sat

Gorilla trekking

An early briefing with traditional dancing, then into the forest with trackers ahead for a regulated, unforgettable hour with a mountain gorilla family. The afternoon is yours to rest, or track the golden monkeys.

Stay: Volcanoes-area lodge · Meals: All

Sun

Return to Kigali & fly home

A relaxed morning, then the scenic drive back to Kigali for your flight home — heart full, weekend well spent.

Meals: Breakfast

All taken care of

Our Story

Curated luxury journeys through East Africa, designed for women.

Home / About us

Rendile Travels was born from a simple belief — that women deserve to experience the wild heart of East Africa on their own terms: safely, beautifully and entirely on their own pace. We design tailor-made journeys through Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda and Rwanda for women who want the magic without the guesswork.

Who we are

We are a boutique, women-focused travel company specialising in East Africa. Our name nods to the Rendille people of northern Kenya — pastoralists known for their resilience, their craftsmanship and their deep connection to the land. That spirit guides how we travel: with respect, with care, and with an eye for the genuinely special. Every itinerary is built by hand, never pulled off a shelf.

Why we travel women-first

Travelling as a woman — solo, with friends, as mothers and daughters, or as a sisterhood — has its own joys and its own considerations. We build for them. That means vetted, welcoming camps, female guides wherever possible, sociable hosted departures for solo travellers, and a Rendile team reachable around the clock. The result is freedom: to wander, to rest, to connect, and to feel completely looked after.

How we travel

We are not a booking engine. Each journey is shaped around you by a designer who knows these countries intimately — your pace, your interests, the way you like to feel at the end of a long, wonderful day. We hand-pick the camps and lodges, arrange every transfer door to door, and stay one step ahead so you never have to. Luxury, to us, is the absence of friction — and the presence of the extraordinary.

Our community

Travel should give back to the places and people that make it possible. We partner with camps and operators who employ and uplift local communities, favour women-led cooperatives for the crafts, coffee and experiences we include, and support the conservation efforts — from gorilla protection to rewilded savannas — that keep East Africa wild. When you travel with us, a meaningful share of your journey stays in the communities you visit.

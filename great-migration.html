The Great Migration

6 days Maasai Mara Jul–Oct · Private or small-group from $6,800 / person
Enquire about this trip
Home / Kenya / The Great Migration

Front-row seats to the greatest wildlife spectacle on earth. From July to October, more than a million wildebeest and zebra brave the crocodile-filled rivers of the Maasai Mara — and you'll watch it unfold from hand-picked camps right on their path.

Six unhurried days in big-cat country, paced for wonder rather than ticking boxes. Fully tailor-made, and lovely as a women-only departure.

Day by day

Your 6-day journey

Day 1

Welcome to Nairobi

Met on arrival and taken to a gracious city hotel to rest and settle in.

Stay: Nairobi hotel · Meals: Dinner

Day 2

Fly to the Maasai Mara

A scenic flight over the Rift Valley, then settle into camp and head out for a golden-hour game drive.

Stay: Luxury tented camp · Meals: All

Day 3

River crossings

With your guide tracking the herds, position for the drama of a Mara River crossing — wildebeest and zebra in their thousands.

Stay: Luxury tented camp · Meals: All

Day 4

Big cats & a balloon dawn

An optional hot-air-balloon flight at sunrise with bush breakfast, then lions, cheetah and leopard through the day.

Stay: Luxury tented camp · Meals: All

Day 5

Conservancy & community

Move to a private conservancy for quieter sightings, a walking safari, and time with a Maasai community on their terms.

Stay: Conservancy camp · Meals: All

Day 6

Return to Nairobi & farewell

Fly back to Nairobi for your onward flight — or continue to Zanzibar for a few barefoot days.

Meals: Breakfast

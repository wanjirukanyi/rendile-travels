Tanzania · Itinerary

Kilimanjaro & the Wild North

9 days Machame route · Ngorongoro · Tarangire Trek & safari from $8,600 / person
Enquire about this trip
Home / Tanzania / Kilimanjaro & the Wild North

Stand on the roof of Africa, then come back down to earth on safari. Six days on the scenic Machame route to Uhuru Peak, fully supported, followed by the elephants of Tarangire and the wildlife of the Ngorongoro Crater.

A bucket-list challenge with a soft landing — our guides, porters and pacing are built around getting you up safely and savouring every step.

Day by day

Your 9-day journey

Day 1

Arrive in Moshi

Met at Kilimanjaro Airport and taken to a comfortable hotel at the mountain's foot for a full kit check and briefing.

Stay: Moshi hotel · Meals: Dinner

Day 2

Machame Gate to Machame Camp

Into the rainforest belt — a lush, gentle first day to ease your legs and lungs into the climb.

Stay: Mountain camp (~3,000m) · Meals: All

Day 3

To Shira Plateau

Out of the forest onto open moorland, with the first big views of the summit ahead.

Stay: Mountain camp (~3,840m) · Meals: All

Day 4

Lava Tower & Barranco

Climb high to Lava Tower then descend to Barranco — "walk high, sleep low" to acclimatise well.

Stay: Barranco camp (~3,960m) · Meals: All

Day 5

Barranco Wall to Karanga

The famous wall scramble, then a shorter day to rest and hydrate before the push for the summit.

Stay: Karanga camp (~3,960m) · Meals: All

Day 6

Summit night

A midnight start under the stars to reach Uhuru Peak (5,895m) at sunrise — the roof of Africa — then a long descent to camp.

Stay: Mweka camp · Meals: All

Day 7

Descend & transfer to Tarangire

Down through the forest to the gate and your summit certificate, then drive to Tarangire to swap boots for binoculars.

Stay: Tarangire lodge · Meals: All

Day 8

Ngorongoro Crater

A full day on the crater floor among lions, rhino and elephant — a triumphant, restful finale.

Stay: Ngorongoro crater-rim lodge · Meals: All

Day 9

Fly home

Transfer to Kilimanjaro Airport for your onward flight, legs aching and heart full.

Meals: Breakfast

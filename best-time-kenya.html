Best Time to Visit Kenya

A month-by-month guide for the perfect safari.

Home / Kenya / Best time to visit

The short answer: Kenya is a wonderful year-round destination, but the dry seasons (late June–October and January–February) offer the best big-game viewing, while July–October brings the Great Migration. Here's how to choose the right month for you.

July – October: the Great Migration & peak season

The dry season and the headline act. Over a million wildebeest and zebra reach the Maasai Mara, with dramatic river crossings usually from August. Wildlife is easy to spot and skies are clear — it's the most popular (and busiest) time, so the best camps book 6–12 months ahead.

January – February: warm, dry & quieter

A lovely, sunny window with excellent big-cat sightings, newborn wildlife and fewer crowds than the migration months. Superb for first-timers and for combining safari with the coast.

March – May: the long rains (green season)

Afternoon showers bring lush green landscapes, baby animals, brilliant birding and the lowest prices. Some remote camps close, and roads can be muddy — but for value and dramatic skies, it's underrated.

June & November: shoulder seasons

Early June (after the rains) and November (the shorter rains) are quiet, green and good value, with the migration herds arriving from late June. Great for travellers who prefer fewer vehicles.

The coast: December – March

For Lamu, Diani and the Swahili coast, December to March is hot, sunny and calm — ideal for beach days and dhow sails. July–October is also lovely and cooler.

When is best for you?

  • Great Migration: July–October
  • Fewer crowds & great value: March–May, November
  • Newborn wildlife & clear skies: January–February
  • Beach & islands: December–March
  • Birdwatching: November–April

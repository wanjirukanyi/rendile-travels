Mummy and Me Safari

5 days Mara · Nairobi Mothers & children from $4,900 / person
Enquire about this trip
Home / Kenya / Mummy and Me Safari

A gentle, joyful first safari designed for mothers and children to share — wildlife encounters by day, hot chocolate and starlit stories by night. Easy distances, family-friendly camps, and guides who are wonderful with little ones.

Five unhurried days that turn into a lifetime memory. Grandmothers, aunties and multi-generational groups are all warmly welcome.

Day by day

Your 5-day journey

Day 1

Welcome to Nairobi

Met on arrival and taken to a family-friendly hotel. An afternoon visit to the Sheldrick elephant orphanage or Giraffe Centre — an instant hit with children.

Stay: Nairobi hotel · Meals: Dinner

Day 2

Fly to the Maasai Mara

A short, exciting flight, then into camp. A relaxed first game drive — giraffe, zebra and elephant make easy, delightful introductions.

Stay: Family tented camp · Meals: All

Day 3

Mara adventures

A morning drive at the children's pace, then back to camp for a swim and a rest. Optional young-ranger activities, spoor-tracking and a Maasai visit.

Stay: Family tented camp · Meals: All

Day 4

Big-cat morning & bush stories

An early drive in search of lions, then afternoon games, a bush picnic and stargazing with the guides after dinner.

Stay: Family tented camp · Meals: All

Day 5

Return to Nairobi & farewell

A final morning in the Mara, then fly back to Nairobi for your onward flight — full hearts and full memory cards.

Meals: Breakfast

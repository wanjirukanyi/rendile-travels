Rwanda · When to go

Best Time to Visit Rwanda

A season-by-season guide to trekking, safari and the green months.

Home / Rwanda / Best time to visit

Rwanda sits just south of the equator, so it's a year-round destination — gorilla permits are issued in every month. What changes with the seasons is how firm the forest trails are, where the game gathers in Akagera, and how green and quiet the country feels. Here's how to choose.

Rwanda's seasons at a glance

There are two drier spells — June–September (the long dry season) and December–February (a shorter dry window) — and two wetter ones, the long rains (March–May) and the short rains (October–November). As an upland, equatorial country, Rwanda is mild and green all year; even in the dry months a forest shower is always possible.

Best for gorilla & chimp trekking

The drier months — June–September and December–February — are ideal for trekking in the Volcanoes National Park and Nyungwe, when the steep trails are firmest. Rwanda's gorilla permits are premium-priced and strictly limited, so we book yours well ahead — especially for these peak windows. Trekking has a minimum age of 15.

Best for safari

For the savanna of Akagera, the dry months concentrate wildlife around the lakes and keep the tracks easy, with June to September especially reliable for big game. The green seasons are lush, scenic and excellent for the park's superb birdlife.

The green seasons

The wetter months (March–May and October–November) bring the deepest greens, dramatic skies, fewer trekkers and often lower lodge rates. Gorilla permits remain available, so flexible travellers who don't mind a muddier hike can enjoy the forest in beautiful solitude.

When to think twice

The heart of the long rains (April–May) can make the forest trails genuinely demanding. For first-time visitors set on a comfortable trek we usually steer toward the dry windows — but with good boots and the right spirit, the green season can be wonderfully atmospheric and rewarding.

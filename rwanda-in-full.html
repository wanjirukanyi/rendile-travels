Rwanda · Itinerary

Rwanda in Full

10 days Volcanoes · Kivu · Nyungwe · Akagera Private & tailor-made from $11,200 / person
Enquire about this trip
Home / Rwanda / Rwanda in Full

The whole of this small, green country in one seamless journey — mountain gorillas in the Volcanoes, the famous canopy walk and chimpanzees of Nyungwe, the Big Five savanna of restored Akagera, and the calm, beautiful shores of Lake Kivu in between.

Rwanda's compact size and superb roads make a grand tour surprisingly relaxed — every drive a scenic ribbon through the land of a thousand hills.

Day by day

Your 10-day journey

Day 1

Arrive in Kigali

Met at the airport for a tour of the capital — the moving Genocide Memorial, the Inema arts centre and a vibrant market.

Stay: Kigali boutique hotel · Meals: Dinner

Day 2

To the Volcanoes

Drive north through terraced hills to the foot of the Virungas, settling into your lodge with the volcanoes on the skyline.

Stay: Volcanoes-area lodge · Meals: All

Day 3

Gorilla trekking

Into the forest with trackers ahead for a magical, regulated hour with a mountain gorilla family.

Stay: Volcanoes-area lodge · Meals: All

Day 4

Golden monkeys & on to Lake Kivu

A morning with the golden monkeys, then a scenic descent to the lakeside town of Gisenyi on the shores of Lake Kivu.

Stay: Lake Kivu lodge · Meals: All

Day 5

Lake Kivu at leisure

A gentle day by one of Africa's great inland seas — a boat to the islands, a coffee-farm visit, or simply rest on the shore.

Stay: Lake Kivu lodge · Meals: All

Day 6

The Congo-Nile trail to Nyungwe

One of Africa's most beautiful drives, hugging the lake then climbing into the ancient rainforest of Nyungwe.

Stay: Nyungwe forest lodge · Meals: All

Day 7

Nyungwe canopy & chimps

An early chimpanzee trek, then the thrilling canopy walkway suspended high above the forest, with views to the hills of Burundi.

Stay: Nyungwe forest lodge · Meals: All

Day 8

East to Akagera

Fly or drive across the country to Akagera National Park in the east — savanna, papyrus lakes and rolling plains, a complete change of scene.

Stay: Akagera tented camp · Meals: All

Day 9

Akagera Big Five

A full day of game drives for lion, elephant, rhino and buffalo, plus an afternoon boat safari among hippo and birds on Lake Ihema.

Stay: Akagera tented camp · Meals: All

Day 10

Return to Kigali & depart

Back to Kigali for a last lunch and your onward flight, having seen the whole of Rwanda.

Meals: Breakfast

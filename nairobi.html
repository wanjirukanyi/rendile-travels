Nairobi & Giraffe Manor

Breakfast with giraffes, baby elephants and a creative city.

Home / Kenya / Nairobi & Giraffe Manor

Most safaris begin and end in Nairobi — and it's worth more than a layover. Kenya's capital is green, creative and surprisingly wild, with a national park on its doorstep, baby elephants to meet, and giraffes that may join you for breakfast.

Giraffe Manor

The famous boutique hotel where resident Rothschild's giraffes poke their heads through the windows at breakfast. It books up far ahead — we secure dates and pair it with a wider Kenya journey.

The Sheldrick Elephant Orphanage

The Sheldrick Wildlife Trust rescues and rehabilitates orphaned elephants. A visit to watch the babies at their mud bath — and learn about conservation — is moving and a highlight for families.

Nairobi National Park

The only national park within a capital city: lions, rhino and giraffe roam against a skyline backdrop. A perfect half-day safari before or after your flight.

Food, culture & shopping

From farm-to-table restaurants and rooftop bars to the Maasai Market and women-led craft collectives, Nairobi rewards a day or two of slow exploring.

Is Nairobi safe for women travellers?

With sensible precautions, yes. We use trusted hotels and private, vetted drivers throughout, with a concierge on call — so you can enjoy the city with confidence.

Where to stay & getting around

We book women-friendly boutique hotels and arrange all private transfers from Jomo Kenyatta International Airport. Nairobi is the hub for flights to every park.

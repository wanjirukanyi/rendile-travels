Tanzania · Itinerary

Classic Northern Circuit

8 days Tarangire · Ngorongoro · Serengeti Private & tailor-made from $8,200 / person
Enquire about this trip
Home / Tanzania / Classic Northern Circuit

The icons of Tanzania's north in one seamless journey — Tarangire's elephants and ancient baobabs, the wildlife-packed amphitheatre of the Ngorongoro Crater, and the endless plains and big cats of the Serengeti.

Private and entirely tailor-made: your own vehicle and guide, unhurried game drives, and hand-picked camps. A wonderful first safari and an easy, rewarding one for women travelling with us.

Day by day

Your 8-day journey

Day 1

Arrive in Arusha

Met at Kilimanjaro Airport and transferred to a leafy lodge near Arusha. Settle in, meet your guide and talk through the days ahead over dinner.

Stay: Arusha country lodge · Meals: Dinner

Day 2

Tarangire National Park

Drive to Tarangire, famous for its great elephant herds and giant baobabs along the river. An afternoon game drive eases you into the rhythm of safari.

Stay: Tarangire tented camp · Meals: All

Day 3

To the Ngorongoro Highlands

Climb through the Rift Valley and coffee-green highlands to the crater rim, with sweeping views over the caldera as you arrive.

Stay: Ngorongoro crater-rim lodge · Meals: All

Day 4

Ngorongoro Crater floor

Descend at dawn into the crater — a natural amphitheatre sheltering lions, rhino, elephant and flamingo-pink soda lakes. A full day of remarkable game viewing.

Stay: Ngorongoro crater-rim lodge · Meals: All

Day 5

Into the Serengeti

Cross the plains into the Serengeti, game viewing as you go. Reach your camp in the central Seronera region in time for a sundowner.

Stay: Serengeti tented camp · Meals: All

Day 6

Serengeti game drives

A full day exploring the heart of the Serengeti — big cats on the kopjes, plains stretching to the horizon. An optional dawn hot-air balloon and bush breakfast.

Stay: Serengeti tented camp · Meals: All

Day 7

Following the wildlife

A flexible day shaped around the season — north toward the migration's river country, or deeper into the plains — with morning and afternoon drives.

Stay: Serengeti tented camp · Meals: All

Day 8

Fly back to Arusha

A last sunrise drive, then a light-aircraft flight over the plains back to Arusha or Kilimanjaro for your onward journey.

Meals: Breakfast

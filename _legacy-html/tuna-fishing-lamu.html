Big-Game Fishing in Lamu

3 days · 2 nights Lamu · The Majlis Resident rates available from $1,650 / person
Enquire about this escape
Home / Off-beat escapes / Big-Game Fishing in Lamu

Something different for the weekend — head to the Lamu archipelago for the deep-blue thrill of big-game fishing, then come back to barefoot Swahili luxury at The Majlis. Chase sailfish, tuna and dorado by day; dhow sails, sundowners and the slow rhythm of the islands by night.

An insider's coast escape for those already in East Africa, with smooth fly-in logistics. Catch-and-release is encouraged; your catch can be cooked for dinner.

Day by day

Your escape

Fri

Fly to Lamu & The Majlis

A flight to Manda, then a short boat hop to The Majlis on the waterfront. Settle in, then a sunset dhow sail across the channel with a cold drink in hand.

Stay: The Majlis, Lamu · Meals: Dinner

Sat

A day on the deep blue

An early start aboard a fully-equipped game-fishing boat with a seasoned skipper — sailfish, yellowfin tuna, dorado and trevally are all on the cards. Lunch on the water, then back to a fresh-seafood feast as the sun drops.

Stay: The Majlis, Lamu · Meals: All

Sun

Stone Town & fly home

A gentle morning — a guided wander through Lamu's UNESCO old town, or one more swim — then the boat and flight home.

Meals: Breakfast

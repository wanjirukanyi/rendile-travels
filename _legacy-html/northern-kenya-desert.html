Northern Kenya Desert Safari

4 days · 3 nights Lake Turkana · Chalbi Frontier expedition from $2,950 / person

Home / Off-beat escapes / Northern Kenya Desert Safari

Few Kenyans, let alone visitors, ever reach the far north — and that is exactly the point. This is a true expedition to the edge of the map: the jade-green waters of Lake Turkana, the shimmering Chalbi Desert, palm oases and the proud cultures of the frontier.

Raw, remote and unforgettable, flown in to save the long roads. For the genuinely adventurous who've "done" the usual parks and want something else entirely.

Day by day

Your expedition

Day 1

Fly to the Jade Sea

A spectacular flight north to Lake Turkana, the world's largest desert lake. Settle into a lodge on the shore at Loiyangalani and watch the wind whip the jade water at dusk.

Stay: Turkana shore lodge · Meals: All

Day 2

Turkana & the El Molo

A boat onto the lake and a visit to the El Molo, one of Africa's smallest peoples, then the singing wells and desert oases of the surrounding frontier.

Stay: Turkana shore lodge · Meals: All

Day 3

Across the Chalbi Desert

Strike out across the vast Chalbi Desert — mirage and salt-pan, camel caravans and the green oasis of Kalacha — for a night under an enormous, star-filled sky.

Stay: Desert camp / Kalacha · Meals: All

Day 4

Fly home

A final desert dawn, then the long, beautiful flight back south over a Kenya few ever see.

Meals: Breakfast

Kitesurfing in Diani

3 days · 2 nights Diani · Galu Beach Resident rates available from $980 / person
Enquire about this escape
Home / Off-beat escapes / Kitesurfing in Diani

Catch the wind on one of the world's loveliest kite spots. During kite season the steady trade winds sweep the wide, flat sands of Galu Beach in Diani — perfect for first lessons or improving your game — and the warm shallow water makes learning a joy.

A high-energy weekend for those already on the coast or in Nairobi, with patient certified instructors and barefoot beach evenings. Total beginners welcome.

Day by day

Your escape

Fri

Fly to Diani

A short flight to Ukunda, then to your laid-back lodge on Galu Beach. Settle in, walk the sand and watch the kites colour the sky at sunset.

Stay: Galu Beach lodge · Meals: Dinner

Sat

On the water

Your kitesurf lessons with an IKO-certified instructor — kite control, body-dragging and your first water starts in the warm lagoon. An afternoon session, then sundowners and tired, happy muscles.

Stay: Galu Beach lodge · Meals: All

Sun

One more session & fly home

A final morning on the kite — or a swim and a long breakfast for the gentler souls — then transfer to the airstrip for your flight home.

Meals: Breakfast

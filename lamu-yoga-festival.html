Lamu Yoga Festival

5 days Lamu & Manda Islands Women & men · adults only from $5,600 / person
Enquire about this trip
Home / Kenya / Lamu Yoga Festival

Kenya's beloved Lamu Yoga & Wellbeing Festival, on the timeless Swahili coast. Five days of beachfront yoga and meditation across the islands of Lamu and Manda, woven with dhow cruises, mangrove swims, Swahili feasts and sunset bonfires.

Over a hundred classes with teachers from around the world, set in a UNESCO-listed old town. Open to women and men (adults only); we handle flights, a beautiful house or boutique stay, and every transfer between islands.

Day by day

Your 5-day journey

Day 1

Fly to Lamu

Fly from Nairobi to the coast and transfer by boat to Shela. Settle into your house, then an evening welcome class and ceremony as the sun goes down.

Stay: Swahili house / boutique hotel · Meals: Dinner

Day 2

Festival days begin

Early-bird beach yoga, then your pick of workshops — movement, meditation, breath-work and sound healing — with time to swim and rest.

Stay: Swahili house / boutique hotel · Meals: Breakfast

Day 3

Dhow & dune

More classes by day; in the evening a dune walk and meditation followed by a Swahili dinner under the stars.

Stay: Swahili house / boutique hotel · Meals: Breakfast

Day 4

Mangroves & celebration

A mangrove swim and sunset dhow cruise, then dance, satsang and a bonfire celebration on the beach.

Stay: Swahili house / boutique hotel · Meals: Breakfast

Day 5

Farewell to Lamu

A final morning class and a wander through Lamu's old town and market before your flight home — or extend with a few days on a private beach.

Meals: Breakfast

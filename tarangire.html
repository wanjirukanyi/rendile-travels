Tarangire: A Woman's Guide

Great herds of elephants among ancient, cathedral-like baobabs.

Home / Tanzania / Tarangire

Tarangire is the quiet star of Tanzania's north — a park of giant baobabs and golden grass where elephants gather by the hundred along the river. It's gloriously uncrowded, wonderfully photogenic, and the perfect gentle opening to a northern-circuit safari.

Why visit Tarangire?

Often overlooked beside the Serengeti and the crater, Tarangire rewards those who linger. In the dry season it holds one of the highest densities of elephants in Tanzania, drawn to the life-giving Tarangire River, and its landscape of ancient baobabs and termite-mound plains is unlike anywhere else on the circuit.

Elephants & ancient baobabs

As the surrounding plains dry, wildlife funnels into the river valley — and the elephant herds are the headline, often a hundred or more at a single bend, mothers and calves among them. Between them stand the baobabs, some over a thousand years old, their swollen trunks scarred where elephants have stripped the bark for moisture.

Wildlife & birds

  • Elephants: the park's signature — large, relaxed breeding herds, especially June to October.
  • Big cats: lion and leopard, with occasional tree-climbing lions and pythons in the dry-season heat.
  • Dry-country specials: fringe-eared oryx, gerenuk and the long-necked giraffe gazelle.
  • Birding capital: over 500 species, including yellow-collared lovebirds and huge flocks of guineafowl.

Best time to visit Tarangire

The dry season, June to October, is prime — wildlife concentrates dramatically along the river and the baobabs glow in low light. The green months bring migrant birds, newborn animals and lush scenery, with fewer vehicles still. Our best time to visit Tanzania guide breaks it down month by month.

Where to stay & how to get there

We choose intimate tented camps and lodges inside and on the fringes of the park, several with elevated suites among the baobabs and views over the valley. Tarangire is only about a two-hour drive from Arusha, which makes it the natural, relaxed first stop before the crater and the Serengeti. We arrange every leg, door to door.

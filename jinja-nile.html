Jinja & the Nile: A Woman's Guide

The source of the world's longest river, and East Africa's adventure capital.

Home / Uganda / Jinja & the Nile

It was here, at Jinja, that explorers finally pinpointed the source of the Nile — the spot where Lake Victoria spills north on a 6,600-kilometre journey to the Mediterranean. Today the river makes Jinja East Africa's adventure capital, from heart-pounding white water to a gentle sunset float.

Why visit Jinja?

A relaxed riverside town a couple of hours east of Kampala, Jinja blends history, adventure and easy charm. Whether you want adrenaline or simply to stand where two great explorers once stood, it's a wonderful change of pace — and a fun, accessible add-on at the start or end of a Uganda journey.

The source of the Nile

A boat trip to the source is a quiet thrill: your guide points out where the lake water visibly wells up to begin the river, with a marker for the spot where Speke confirmed the source in 1862. It's a low-key but genuinely moving moment — the beginning of the longest river on earth.

Things to do

  • White-water rafting: world-class grade-five rapids for the brave, with calmer family floats too.
  • On the water: kayaking, stand-up paddleboarding and serene sunset cruises.
  • On land: quad biking, mountain biking, and visits to local craft and community projects.

Best time to visit

Jinja is enjoyable year-round; the river runs regardless of season. The drier months — June–September and December–February — are most comfortable for time on the water. See our best time to visit Uganda guide for more.

Where to stay & how to get there

We favour lodges set in gardens right above the river, with the sound of the rapids below. Jinja is an easy drive from Entebbe or Kampala, making it a natural first or last stop — and a brilliant, gentle adventure for families on our Uganda Family Adventure. We arrange every transfer.

Hot-Air Balloon over the Mara

2 days · 1 night Maasai Mara · Fly-in Resident rates available from $1,290 / person
Enquire about this escape
Home / Off-beat escapes / Hot-Air Balloon over the Mara

A bucket-list moment in a single overnight. Drift in silence over the Maasai Mara at first light, the plains and their herds unrolling beneath you, then touch down to a champagne breakfast laid out in the bush.

The fastest, most romantic way to fall in love with the Mara — perfect for an anniversary, a birthday, or simply because it's the weekend.

Day by day

Your escape

Day 1

Fly to the Mara

A midday flight from Nairobi to a Mara airstrip, into camp for lunch, and an afternoon game drive in search of lions and elephants as the light softens.

Stay: Mara tented camp · Meals: All

Day 2

Dawn balloon & fly home

A pre-dawn start to lift off as the sun rises — an hour drifting over the plains, then a champagne bush breakfast on landing. A last game drive, and the midday flight home.

Meals: Breakfast

All taken care of

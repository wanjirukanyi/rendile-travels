Uganda Family Adventure

6 days Jinja · Lake Mburo · Entebbe Mothers & children from $4,600 / person
Enquire about this trip
Home / Uganda / Uganda Family Adventure

A gentler mix of wildlife and water, paced for families — the source of the mighty Nile at Jinja, boat and walking safaris at Lake Mburo, and a moving morning with rescued chimpanzees on an island sanctuary.

Short distances, plenty of activity and lodges with room to roam. (Gorilla and chimp forest treks have a minimum age of 15, so this journey keeps younger children happily in the thick of it instead.)

Day by day

Your 6-day journey

Day 1

Arrive in Entebbe

Met at the airport and taken to a family-friendly lake-shore hotel with gardens and a pool to unwind after the flight.

Stay: Entebbe lake hotel · Meals: Dinner

Day 2

Jinja & the source of the Nile

Drive to Jinja, the adventure capital, where the Nile begins its long journey north. A gentle family boat trip to the source, plus optional quad biking or a calm-water float.

Stay: Jinja river lodge · Meals: All

Day 3

To Lake Mburo

A scenic drive south-west, crossing the equator (a fun photo stop and science lesson) to Lake Mburo National Park.

Stay: Lake Mburo lodge · Meals: All

Day 4

Lake Mburo safari

A boat safari for hippo and birds, plus a guided nature walk among zebra, giraffe and impala — Mburo is one of the few parks where walking with wildlife is allowed.

Stay: Lake Mburo lodge · Meals: All

Day 5

Return & Ngamba chimps

Back to Entebbe, then a boat to Ngamba Island sanctuary to meet rescued chimpanzees from the viewing platform — an unforgettable hour for all ages.

Stay: Entebbe lake hotel · Meals: All

Day 6

Farewell

A relaxed morning by the lake, then transfer to the airport for your onward flight — full hearts and full memory cards.

Meals: Breakfast

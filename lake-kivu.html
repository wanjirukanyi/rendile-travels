Lake Kivu: A Woman's Guide

Beaches, islands and easy lakeside towns on one of Africa's great inland seas.

Home / Rwanda / Lake Kivu

After the effort of a gorilla trek or a forest hike, Lake Kivu is where Rwanda slows down. One of the great lakes of Africa, its blue waters are ringed by terraced hills and easy lakeside towns — a place to swim, sail to an island, sip local coffee and simply breathe.

Why visit Lake Kivu?

Forming much of Rwanda's western border with the DR Congo, Lake Kivu is calm, safe and free of the bilharzia and large predators of many African lakes, so swimming and paddling are a pleasure. Its lakeside resort towns — Gisenyi (Rubavu), Kibuye (Karongi) and Rusizi — make a relaxed, scenic interlude between the volcanoes and the rainforest.

Things to do

  • Island-hopping: boat trips to forested islands, fishing villages and quiet swimming coves.
  • Coffee & culture: tour a lakeside coffee washing station and meet the women's cooperatives behind Rwanda's celebrated beans.
  • On the water: kayaking, stand-up paddleboarding and watching the fishermen sing their way out at dusk.

The Congo-Nile trail

The road and trail hugging the lake's eastern shore — the Congo-Nile trail — is one of the most beautiful journeys in Africa, winding past terraced hills, tea estates and tiny harbours. We build a stretch of it into journeys that link the volcanoes with Nyungwe, turning the transfer itself into a highlight.

Best time to visit

Lake Kivu is lovely year-round, but it shines in the drier months — June–September and December–February — when the days are warm and clear for time on the water. The green seasons bring misty, romantic mornings over the lake. See our best time to visit Rwanda guide for more.

Where to stay & how to get there

We favour calm, characterful lodges with gardens running down to the shore and their own little beaches and jetties. Lake Kivu is an easy drive from the Volcanoes National Park or Nyungwe, making it the natural restful heart of a Rwanda journey. We arrange every leg and any boat excursions.

Murchison Falls: A Woman's Guide

The Nile forced through a seven-metre gorge, then a wildlife-rich delta.

Home / Uganda / Murchison Falls

Uganda's largest national park is built around one of the most powerful sights on the Nile — the entire river squeezed through a gorge just seven metres wide, exploding into a thunder of spray and rainbows. Around it spreads classic big-game savanna, best explored by both vehicle and boat.

Why visit Murchison Falls?

Murchison combines a genuine natural wonder with excellent wildlife. North of the river, golden savanna rolls toward the horizon with elephant, giraffe and lion; south lies woodland and the road to the falls themselves. The mighty Victoria Nile runs through the middle, making this one of the most scenic parks in East Africa.

The falls & the Nile boat safari

The signature experience is an afternoon boat cruise upriver to the base of the falls. You glide past hippo pods, enormous crocodiles basking on the banks, elephant and buffalo coming down to drink, and clouds of waterbirds — ending at the foot of the roaring cascade. Many guests then walk to the top of the falls for the view straight down the gorge.

Wildlife you'll see

  • Big game: elephant, lion, leopard, buffalo and the towering Rothschild's giraffe.
  • River life: hippo and Nile crocodile in great numbers along the water.
  • Birds: over 450 species, including the prehistoric-looking shoebill in the Nile delta.

Best time to visit

The dry months — June–September and December–February — draw wildlife to the river and make game drives easiest. The green seasons are lush and quieter, with superb birding. See our best time to visit Uganda guide for more.

Where to stay & how to get there

We choose riverside lodges with the Nile on the doorstep and the sound of the water at night. Murchison is reached by light aircraft from Entebbe or as a scenic drive north, often paired with a walking encounter at the Ziwa rhino sanctuary on the way. We arrange every transfer and the boat cruise.

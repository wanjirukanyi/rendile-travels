Tanzania Family Safari

6 days Tarangire · Manyara · Ngorongoro Mothers & children from $5,200 / person
Enquire about this trip
Home / Tanzania / Tanzania Family Safari

A gentle northern loop made for families — short, easy drives, lots of elephants, and lodges with pools and room to roam. Wildlife by day; hot chocolate and starlit stories by night.

Designed around children's energy and attention spans, with guides who are wonderful with little ones. Grandmothers, aunties and multi-generational groups are all warmly welcome.

Day by day

Your 6-day journey

Day 1

Arrive in Arusha

Met at Kilimanjaro Airport and taken to a family-friendly lodge with a pool and gardens to stretch out after the flight.

Stay: Arusha country lodge · Meals: Dinner

Day 2

Tarangire National Park

A short drive to Tarangire, where huge elephant herds and giant baobabs make an instant, delightful introduction to safari.

Stay: Tarangire family lodge · Meals: All

Day 3

Lake Manyara

An easy day at Manyara — flamingo-pink shallows, playful baboon troops and the famous tree-climbing lions, with a relaxed picnic by the lake.

Stay: Karatu highlands lodge · Meals: All

Day 4

Ngorongoro Crater

Down into the crater for a half-day of easy, big-reward game viewing — lions, elephant and rhino — then back up for an afternoon by the fire.

Stay: Karatu highlands lodge · Meals: All

Day 5

Maasai morning & free time

A friendly visit to a Maasai village — beadwork, songs and stories the children love — then a relaxed afternoon to swim and rest.

Stay: Karatu highlands lodge · Meals: All

Day 6

Return & farewell

An easy drive back to Arusha or Kilimanjaro for your onward flight — full hearts and full memory cards.

Meals: Breakfast

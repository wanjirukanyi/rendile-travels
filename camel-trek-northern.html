Camel Trek in the Northern Frontier

3 days · 2 nights Samburu · Northern Kenya Walking safari from $1,550 / person
Enquire about this escape
Home / Off-beat escapes / Camel Trek in the Northern Frontier

Slow right down to the pace of a camel. In Kenya's wild northern frontier you walk with Samburu warriors and their camels through acacia country, far from any road or crowd, and sleep out under a sky thick with stars.

A soulful, screen-free reset for the adventurous — the camels carry the camp, you carry nothing but a daypack, and the silence does the rest.

Day by day

Your escape

Fri

Into the frontier

Fly north to Samburu country and meet your guides and camels. A gentle first walk into the bush to your fly-camp, then a fireside dinner under the stars.

Stay: Mobile fly-camp · Meals: All

Sat

A day on foot

Walk with the camels through dry riverbeds and acacia woodland, tracking wildlife and learning the bush from the Samburu. A long lunch in the shade, then a new camp as the light turns gold.

Stay: Mobile fly-camp · Meals: All

Sun

Last walk & fly home

A cool dawn walk, breakfast in the bush, then the flight back south — dusty, unplugged and quietly transformed.

Meals: Breakfast

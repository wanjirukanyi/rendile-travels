Big Game & Lake Kivu

6 days Akagera · Lake Kivu Private from $6,200 / person
Enquire about this trip
Home / Rwanda / Big Game & Lake Kivu

The other side of Rwanda — beyond the gorillas lies a classic Big Five safari. Restored Akagera, on the eastern border, now teems with lion, elephant and rhino across rolling savanna and papyrus lakes, while the calm shores of Lake Kivu offer the perfect, restful counterpoint.

A wonderful gorilla-free safari in its own right, or an easy add-on for those who want savanna as well as forest.

Day by day

Your 6-day journey

Day 1

Arrive in Kigali

Met at the airport for a short city tour — the moving Genocide Memorial and a vibrant craft market — and an overnight in the capital.

Stay: Kigali boutique hotel · Meals: Dinner

Day 2

To Akagera

An easy drive east to Akagera National Park, with an afternoon game drive through golden savanna as the light softens.

Stay: Akagera tented camp · Meals: All

Day 3

Akagera Big Five

A full day of game drives in search of lion, elephant, buffalo and reintroduced rhino, plus a relaxed afternoon boat safari among hippo and birds on Lake Ihema.

Stay: Akagera tented camp · Meals: All

Day 4

West to Lake Kivu

A scenic transfer across the country (or a quick connecting flight) to the lakeside town of Gisenyi on the shores of Lake Kivu.

Stay: Lake Kivu lodge · Meals: All

Day 5

Lake Kivu at leisure

A gentle day by the lake — a boat to the islands, a coffee-farm visit, swimming and sundowners over the water.

Stay: Lake Kivu lodge · Meals: All

Day 6

Return to Kigali & depart

A scenic drive back to Kigali for a last lunch and your onward flight.

Meals: Breakfast

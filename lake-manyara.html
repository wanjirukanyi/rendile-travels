Lake Manyara: A Woman's Guide

Flamingo-pink shallows, giraffes and famous tree-climbing lions.

Home / Tanzania / Lake Manyara

Small but wonderfully varied, Lake Manyara packs a lot into a single day — a silver soda lake fringed with flamingos, a cool groundwater forest, and the famous lions that drape themselves along the acacia branches. It's the perfect, gentle counterpoint between Tarangire and the Ngorongoro Crater.

Why visit Lake Manyara?

Tucked beneath the dramatic wall of the Rift Valley escarpment, Manyara compresses an unusual range of habitats — forest, floodplain, lake and woodland — into a slim, scenic park. That diversity means a lot to see in a short, easy visit, which is why it slots so neatly into the classic northern circuit.

The tree-climbing lions

Manyara is one of only a few places in Africa where lions habitually rest in the trees, lounging along the limbs of acacias and sausage trees in the heat of the day. No one is quite sure why they do it — shade, breeze, or simply a better view — but spotting a pride aloft is a safari highlight you'll never forget.

Wildlife & birds

  • Flamingos & waterbirds: the lake can shimmer pink with flamingos, alongside pelicans, storks and herons.
  • Forest dwellers: large troops of baboon and blue monkey, plus elephant moving through the groundwater forest.
  • Plains & lakeshore: giraffe, zebra, buffalo, hippo and bushbuck.
  • Birding gem: over 400 recorded species in a compact area.

Best time to visit

The dry season (June–October) is best for general game viewing and the tree-climbing lions; the wetter months (November–June) bring lush scenery, migrant birds and the largest flamingo gatherings. Either way, Manyara is an easy half- or full-day visit. See our best time to visit Tanzania guide for more.

Where to stay & how to get there

We favour lodges perched on the Rift escarpment with long views over the lake, or characterful camps in the nearby Karatu highlands. Manyara is about a two-hour drive from Arusha and sits directly on the road to Ngorongoro, so it pairs perfectly with the crater. Optional night drives, a treetop canopy walkway and hot springs add to the day.

Rwanda · Itinerary

Primates of Rwanda

7 days Volcanoes · Lake Kivu · Nyungwe Private & tailor-made from $8,900 / person
Enquire about this trip
Home / Rwanda / Primates of Rwanda

Rwanda's two great primate experiences in one journey — mountain gorillas in the misty Virungas, and chimpanzees and the famous canopy walk in the ancient rainforest of Nyungwe — linked by the beautiful shores of Lake Kivu.

Private and tailor-made, with exceptional lodges and some of the most scenic drives in Africa. Trekking is suitable for reasonably fit travellers aged 15 and over.

Day by day

Your 7-day journey

Day 1

Arrive in Kigali

Met at the airport for a tour of the capital — the moving Genocide Memorial and a buzzing craft market — before driving north toward the Volcanoes.

Stay: Volcanoes-area lodge · Meals: Dinner

Day 2

Gorilla trekking

Into the forest with trackers ahead for a magical, regulated hour with a mountain gorilla family in the Volcanoes National Park.

Stay: Volcanoes-area lodge · Meals: All

Day 3

Golden monkeys & to Lake Kivu

A morning with the golden monkeys, then a scenic descent to Lake Kivu for a restful evening on the shore.

Stay: Lake Kivu lodge · Meals: All

Day 4

The Congo-Nile trail to Nyungwe

One of Africa's most beautiful drives along the lake, then up into the ancient rainforest of Nyungwe.

Stay: Nyungwe forest lodge · Meals: All

Day 5

Chimpanzee trekking

An early start to track a community of wild chimpanzees through the forest, following their calls into the canopy.

Stay: Nyungwe forest lodge · Meals: All

Day 6

The canopy walk

Walk the thrilling suspended canopy bridge high above the forest, then a guided trail for colobus monkeys and superb birds.

Stay: Nyungwe forest lodge · Meals: All

Day 7

Return to Kigali & depart

Fly or drive back to Kigali for a last lunch and your onward flight.

Meals: Breakfast

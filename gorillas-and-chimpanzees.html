Gorillas & Chimpanzees

7 days Kibale · Bwindi Private & tailor-made from $6,400 / person
Enquire about this trip
Home / Uganda / Gorillas & Chimpanzees

The two great-ape encounters of a lifetime in one journey — an hour with a mountain gorilla family in the misty Bwindi forest, and a morning among wild chimpanzees in Kibale, the primate capital of East Africa.

Private and tailor-made, with comfortable lodges and a guide who knows the forests intimately. Gorilla trekking is suitable for reasonably fit travellers aged 15 and over.

Day by day

Your 7-day journey

Day 1

Arrive in Entebbe

Met at the airport and taken to a tranquil lake-shore hotel to rest after your flight and meet your guide.

Stay: Entebbe lake hotel · Meals: Dinner

Day 2

To the Kibale forest

Fly or drive west to Kibale, through tea estates and crater-lake country, arriving at your forest lodge in time for a sundowner.

Stay: Kibale forest lodge · Meals: All

Day 3

Chimpanzee trekking

A morning tracking habituated chimpanzees through the canopy — hooting calls, drumming on buttress roots, families overhead. An afternoon Bigodi wetland walk for monkeys and birds.

Stay: Kibale forest lodge · Meals: All

Day 4

South to Bwindi

A scenic drive south through Queen Elizabeth's plains and the terraced hills to the edge of the Bwindi Impenetrable Forest.

Stay: Bwindi forest lodge · Meals: All

Day 5

Gorilla trekking in Bwindi

The day you came for. With trackers ahead, you hike into the ancient forest to spend a magical, regulated hour with a mountain gorilla family — silverback, mothers and tumbling youngsters.

Stay: Bwindi forest lodge · Meals: All

Day 6

Bwindi at leisure

A gentler day — a Batwa cultural experience or community walk, or a second gorilla permit for those who can't get enough. Time to simply soak up the forest.

Stay: Bwindi forest lodge · Meals: All

Day 7

Fly to Entebbe & depart

A light-aircraft flight back to Entebbe for your onward connection, with the forest still in your ears.

Meals: Breakfast

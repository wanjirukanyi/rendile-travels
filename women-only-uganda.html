Women-Only Uganda Gorilla Journey

8 days Queen Elizabeth · Bwindi · Lake Bunyonyi Hosted group of 8–12 from $7,200 / person
Enquire about this trip
Home / Uganda / Women-Only Uganda Gorilla Journey

Our sisterhood departure to the heart of the "pearl of Africa" — the lions and elephants of Queen Elizabeth and an unforgettable hour with the mountain gorillas of Bwindi, in an intimate group of 8–12 women led by a female host.

Vetted lodges, thoughtful pacing and real connection — including time with the women's groups our community partners support. Come solo and leave with friends.

Day by day

Your 8-day journey

Day 1

Arrive & gather in Entebbe

Met at the airport and welcomed to a lake-shore hotel, where the group comes together over a welcome dinner with your host.

Stay: Entebbe lake hotel · Meals: Dinner

Day 2

Fly to Queen Elizabeth

A light-aircraft flight west to Queen Elizabeth National Park, with an afternoon game drive among elephant, buffalo and kob.

Stay: Queen Elizabeth lodge · Meals: All

Day 3

Kazinga & tree-climbing lions

A morning game drive in search of the Ishasha tree-climbing lions, then an afternoon cruise on the wildlife-lined Kazinga Channel.

Stay: Queen Elizabeth lodge · Meals: All

Day 4

South to Bwindi

A scenic drive into the terraced hills to the edge of the Bwindi Impenetrable Forest, settling in for the encounter to come.

Stay: Bwindi forest lodge · Meals: All

Day 5

Gorilla trekking in Bwindi

The day you came for — a hike into the ancient forest with trackers ahead, and a magical, regulated hour with a mountain gorilla family.

Stay: Bwindi forest lodge · Meals: All

Day 6

Community & women's groups

A warm morning with a Bwindi women's cooperative — weaving, tea and shared stories — and an optional second gorilla permit for those who wish.

Stay: Bwindi forest lodge · Meals: All

Day 7

Lake Bunyonyi

A short drive to the terraced shores of Lake Bunyonyi — a restful day of canoes, birdsong and reflection together.

Stay: Lake Bunyonyi lodge · Meals: All

Day 8

Fly home together

A morning by the lake, then a light-aircraft flight back to Entebbe — new friendships and a fuller heart for the journey home.

Meals: Breakfast

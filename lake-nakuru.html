Lake Nakuru & the Great Rift

Flamingo-pink shores and rhino in the Rift Valley.

Home / Kenya / Lake Nakuru & the Rift

A shimmering soda lake fringed with pink, set in the dramatic floor of the Great Rift Valley — Lake Nakuru is one of Kenya's most beautiful and easily reached parks, and a rhino stronghold to boot.

Why visit Lake Nakuru?

Lake Nakuru National Park, in Kenya's central Rift Valley about three hours from Nairobi, is compact, scenic and brimming with wildlife. It slots neatly into a classic safari between the Mara and the rest of the country, adding rhino, flamingos and Rift Valley grandeur.

The flamingos

Lake Nakuru is famous for the flocks of lesser and greater flamingos that can turn its shallows pink. Numbers rise and fall with water levels, but the sight of thousands of birds against the soda flats is unforgettable. Nearby Lake Bogoria is another superb flamingo spot we can add.

A rhino sanctuary

The park is a fenced sanctuary protecting both black and white rhino, giving some of Kenya's most reliable rhino sightings — a moving encounter with one of Africa's most endangered animals.

Other wildlife

  • Rothschild's giraffe, buffalo and waterbuck
  • Lion and leopard (often in the yellow-bark acacias)
  • Over 450 bird species

Best time to visit

Year-round, with the drier months (June–October, January–March) best for game viewing. Flamingo numbers vary with rainfall, so we'll advise on current conditions. See our best time to visit Kenya guide.

Where to stay

We choose women-friendly lodges with Rift Valley views, from elegant cliff-top retreats to lakeside lodges, all vetted for warmth and safety.

How to get there

An easy, scenic road transfer from Nairobi (about three hours) via the Great Rift Valley viewpoints, or a short charter flight. We arrange every detail.

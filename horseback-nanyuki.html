Horseback Safari in Nanyuki

3 days · 2 nights Nanyuki · Laikipia All riding abilities from $1,750 / person
Enquire about this escape
Home / Off-beat escapes / Horseback Safari in Nanyuki

There's no closer way to the wild than from the saddle. On the rolling Laikipia plains around Nanyuki you ride right alongside zebra, giraffe and gazelle — no engine, no fences — with Mount Kenya rising snow-tipped on the horizon.

A horse-lover's weekend (and a wonderful first ride for novices), with sure-footed, well-schooled horses, expert guides and a private conservancy all to yourself.

Day by day

Your escape

Fri

To Nanyuki & the saddle

Fly or drive up to Laikipia, crossing the equator on the way. Meet your horse and guide for a gentle afternoon ride to find your seat, then sundowners over the plains.

Stay: Laikipia riding lodge · Meals: All

Sat

A full day in the saddle

A longer ride across the conservancy, cantering beside plains game and pausing for a bush picnic in the shade. An optional walk with rhino or a sundowner ride to close the day.

Stay: Laikipia riding lodge · Meals: All

Sun

Morning ride & home

One last dawn ride with the mountain glowing pink, a leisurely breakfast, then the journey home.

Meals: Breakfast

All taken care of

A Weekend in the Mara

3 days · 2 nights Maasai Mara · Fly-in Resident rates available from $1,490 / person
Enquire about this escape
Home / Quick escapes / A Weekend in the Mara

Already in East Africa? Trade the city for the plains for a single, glorious weekend. Fly into the Maasai Mara on Friday, spend two unhurried nights in big-cat country, and be home by Sunday evening — refreshed and full of stories.

Designed for residents and short-stay travellers, with smooth fly-in logistics and special resident rates. Easily extended to a third night.

Day by day

Your weekend

Fri

Fly to the Mara

A late-morning flight from Nairobi's Wilson Airport drifts you over the Rift Valley to a Mara airstrip in about 45 minutes. Settle into camp, then an afternoon game drive and sundowners as the light turns gold.

Stay: Mara tented camp · Meals: Dinner

Sat

A full day on the plains

A dawn drive in search of lions and cheetah, an optional hot-air balloon with champagne bush breakfast, and an afternoon drive — with a Maasai village visit if you wish.

Stay: Mara tented camp · Meals: All

Sun

Morning drive & fly home

One last sunrise drive and a leisurely brunch, then a midday flight back to Nairobi — home in time for Sunday evening.

Meals: Breakfast

Why Kenya

Where safari was born.

Kenya is where the word safari began — and it still does it best. In one journey you can drift over the Maasai Mara at dawn in a hot-air balloon, watch elephants graze beneath Mount Kilimanjaro, and end with your toes in the warm Indian Ocean.

For women travelling with us, Kenya is also wonderfully easy: superb camps, warm hospitality, female guides where possible, and a Rendile team on the ground every step of the way.

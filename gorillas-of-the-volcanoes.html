Gorillas of the Volcanoes

4 days Volcanoes National Park Private from $5,400 / person
Enquire about this trip
Home / Rwanda / Gorillas of the Volcanoes

The quickest, most luxurious route to a life-list encounter — fly into Kigali and drive a smooth two hours to the Volcanoes National Park, where you'll spend a magical hour with a mountain gorilla family in the misty Virunga foothills.

Rwanda makes gorilla trekking wonderfully easy and refined: short transfers, exceptional lodges and a seamless permit process. Trekking is suitable for reasonably fit travellers aged 15 and over.

Day by day

Your 4-day journey

Day 1

Arrive in Kigali

Met at the airport for a short tour of Rwanda's clean, creative capital — the moving Genocide Memorial and a buzzing craft market — before driving north to your lodge near the Volcanoes National Park.

Stay: Volcanoes-area lodge · Meals: Dinner

Day 2

Gorilla trekking

An early briefing at park headquarters, then into the bamboo and forest with trackers ahead. The moment you reach the family — a great silverback at rest, mothers and tumbling youngsters — is unforgettable. The afternoon is yours to rest and reflect.

Stay: Volcanoes-area lodge · Meals: All

Day 3

Golden monkeys & the foothills

Track the playful, endangered golden monkeys through the bamboo, then choose a hike to the Dian Fossey tomb, the twin lakes, or a warm cultural visit to a local community.

Stay: Volcanoes-area lodge · Meals: All

Day 4

Return to Kigali & depart

A scenic drive back through the terraced hills to Kigali for your onward flight.

Meals: Breakfast

Savanna to Sea

10 days Maasai Mara · The Coast Safari & beach from $9,400 / person
Enquire about this trip
Home / Kenya / Savanna to Sea

The perfect first, full taste of Kenya: thrilling big-cat days in the Maasai Mara, then a slow exhale on the warm Indian Ocean. Ten days that balance adventure and rest — equally lovely for couples, friends, or a women-only group.

We can set the beach finale on Diani, Lamu, or hop across to Zanzibar. Fully tailor-made throughout.

Day by day

Your 10-day journey

Day 1

Welcome to Nairobi

Met on arrival and taken to a gracious city hotel to rest and settle in.

Stay: Nairobi hotel · Meals: Dinner

Days 2–4

Maasai Mara

Fly to the Mara for three full days of big cats, plains game and (in season) the Great Migration — with an optional balloon dawn and a Maasai community visit.

Stay: Luxury tented camp · Meals: All

Day 5

Amboseli & Kilimanjaro

Fly to Amboseli for elephants framed by Africa's highest peak — one of the continent's great views.

Stay: Amboseli lodge · Meals: All

Day 6

To the coast

Fly to the Indian Ocean and transfer to your beach house. Toes in the sand by sunset.

Stay: Beach house / boutique hotel · Meals: Breakfast

Days 7–9

Barefoot days by the sea

Three unhurried days: swim, snorkel, sail a dhow at sunset, spa mornings, Swahili dinners — exactly as much or as little as you wish.

Stay: Beach house / boutique hotel · Meals: Breakfast

Day 10

Farewell

A final morning by the ocean, then transfer for your flight home, rested and glowing.

Meals: Breakfast

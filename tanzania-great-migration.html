The Great Migration

7 days Serengeti · Jun–Oct Private or small-group from $7,400 / person
Enquire about this trip
Home / Tanzania / The Great Migration

Front-row seats to the greatest wildlife spectacle on earth — more than a million wildebeest and zebra massing on the banks of the Mara River, then plunging across in a thunder of dust and water.

We place you in intimate camps right on the herds' seasonal path, with a guide who reads the plains and the patience to wait for the moment. Timed for the crossings, roughly June to October.

Day by day

Your 7-day journey

Day 1

Fly to the northern Serengeti

Arrive at Kilimanjaro and connect by light aircraft to the northern Serengeti, landing close to the migration's river country. An afternoon drive among the gathering herds.

Stay: Northern Serengeti migration camp · Meals: All from lunch

Day 2

Mara River country

Morning and afternoon drives tracking the wildebeest toward the river, reading their restless movement as pressure builds at the crossing points.

Stay: Northern Serengeti migration camp · Meals: All

Day 3

The crossings

A full, flexible day positioned at the river. When the moment comes the herds surge across in their thousands — heart-stopping, and worth every patient hour.

Stay: Northern Serengeti migration camp · Meals: All

Day 4

Predators & plains

Where the herds go, the big cats follow. Lion prides, cheetah on the termite mounds and hyena clans — the drama that trails the migration.

Stay: Northern Serengeti migration camp · Meals: All

Day 5

South to the central plains

Transfer toward the central Serengeti, game viewing along the way. An optional dawn hot-air balloon drifts you over the savanna with a champagne bush breakfast to land.

Stay: Central Serengeti camp · Meals: All

Day 6

Serengeti at leisure

Unhurried morning and afternoon drives among the kopjes and rivers of the Seronera, with time to simply sit with a sighting.

Stay: Central Serengeti camp · Meals: All

Day 7

Fly home

A final sunrise drive, then a light-aircraft flight back to Kilimanjaro for your onward connection.

Meals: Breakfast

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to plan an East Africa journey with confidence.

Home / FAQs

A few of the questions we're asked most often. Don't see yours? Get in touch — your travel designer is always happy to help.

Is East Africa safe for women travellers?

Yes. Thousands of women — solo, with friends, mothers and daughters — safari in East Africa every year. On safari you are with a professional guide at all times, in vetted camps and lodges, with a Rendile concierge reachable around the clock. We choose trusted properties, arrange private transfers, and pace every journey around how you want to feel.

Do I have to travel in a group?

Not at all. Most of our journeys are private and tailor-made — just you and your travelling companions, your own guide and vehicle. For solo travellers who'd like company, we also run a small number of hosted women-only departures in intimate groups of 8–12, led by a female host.

How do gorilla and chimpanzee permits work?

Mountain-gorilla and chimpanzee trekking require permits, which are strictly limited and often sell out months ahead in peak season. We secure yours as part of your booking. Trekking has a minimum age of 15 and suits reasonably fit travellers; porters are available and warmly recommended. Rwanda's gorilla permit is premium-priced; Uganda's is more moderate.

When is the best time to visit?

It depends on what you most want to see — the Great Migration, calving season, gorilla trekking or the beach. Broadly, June–October and December–February are the popular dry windows. See our country guides: Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda and Rwanda.

What's included in the price?

Our quoted prices typically include your accommodation, internal flights and transfers, game drives and activities, park and conservation fees, the meals shown in the itinerary, and our 24/7 concierge. International flights, visas, travel insurance and personal extras are usually additional. Every itinerary lists exactly what's included so there are no surprises.

Can you arrange family trips?

Absolutely — we love designing gentle, joyful family safaris with short drives, family-friendly camps and guides who are wonderful with children. Note that gorilla and chimpanzee trekking have a minimum age of 15, so for younger families we focus on big-game safaris and other adventures.

How do payments and deposits work?

A deposit secures your journey and any time-sensitive permits and flights, with the balance due before you travel. We'll set out the exact schedule, accepted payment methods and our flexible-rebooking terms in writing before you commit — clearly and without jargon.

Do I need visas and vaccinations?

Most travellers need a visa (often available online or on arrival), and a yellow-fever certificate is required for parts of the region. We'll send country-specific guidance for your itinerary and recommend you confirm health requirements with a travel clinic. We're happy to help you prepare.

How far ahead should I book?

For peak-season travel and gorilla trekking, six to twelve months ahead is ideal, as the best camps and permits are limited. That said, we can often arrange wonderful journeys at shorter notice — just ask.

Are your itineraries fixed?

No — every itinerary on this site is a starting point, not a fixed menu. Tell us your dates, pace and interests and we'll reshape any journey entirely around you, or design something new from scratch.

Still have a question?

Your travel designer is happy to talk it through — no pressure, no obligation.

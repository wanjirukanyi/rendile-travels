Kigali: A Woman's Guide

A clean, creative and deeply moving capital — and the gateway to Rwanda.

Home / Rwanda / Kigali

Most visitors arrive in Kigali expecting only a gateway to the gorillas — and leave wishing they'd stayed longer. Rwanda's capital is famously clean, calm and safe, with a thoughtful arts and food scene, warm people and a moving history that rewards a day or two of your time.

Why spend time in Kigali?

Spread over a series of green ridges, Kigali is among the tidiest and safest cities in Africa — the result of strong civic pride and the famous monthly community clean-up, umuganda. It's an easy, welcoming place to land and acclimatise, and the perfect bookend to a safari, with excellent hotels and restaurants.

The Genocide Memorial

The Kigali Genocide Memorial is the most important place to visit — a dignified, deeply moving museum and resting place that honours the more than 250,000 victims buried there, and tells the story of the 1994 genocide against the Tutsi with honesty and care. It's a sobering but essential part of understanding the remarkable country Rwanda has become.

Things to do

  • Arts & design: the colourful Inema Arts Centre and Niyo gallery, full of contemporary Rwandan work.
  • Women-led tours: the Nyamirambo Women's Centre runs walking, cooking and craft experiences in a lively neighbourhood.
  • Markets & coffee: the bustling Kimironko market and Kigali's superb third-wave coffee shops.

Is Kigali safe for women travellers?

Yes — Kigali is consistently rated one of the safest capitals in Africa, and many women travel here solo with confidence. As anywhere, we arrange trusted guides and private transfers, and our concierge is reachable around the clock. It's a reassuring, easy place to begin or end your journey.

Where to stay & how to get there

We favour leafy boutique hotels and refined city retreats with pools and gardens. Kigali International Airport is well connected across Africa and beyond, and from the city it's a smooth two-to-three-hour drive to the volcanoes or Akagera. We arrange every transfer and tour.

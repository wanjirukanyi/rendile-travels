Tanzania · Itinerary

Serengeti & Zanzibar

10 days Serengeti · Zanzibar Safari & beach from $9,800 / person
Enquire about this trip
Home / Tanzania / Serengeti & Zanzibar

The perfect full taste of Tanzania — big cats and golden plains in the Serengeti and the Ngorongoro Crater, then a short flight to the spice island for barefoot days, dhow sails and the warm Indian Ocean.

Adventure first, then deep rest: it's the combination guests remember most, and it's easily paced to suit honeymooners, friends and solo travellers alike.

Day by day

Your 10-day journey

Day 1

Arrive in Arusha

Met at Kilimanjaro Airport and taken to a tranquil lodge near Arusha to settle in and meet your guide.

Stay: Arusha country lodge · Meals: Dinner

Day 2

Tarangire National Park

Drive to Tarangire for an afternoon among its elephant herds and ancient baobabs.

Stay: Tarangire tented camp · Meals: All

Day 3

Ngorongoro Crater

Up to the highlands, then a full day on the crater floor — lions, rhino, elephant and flamingo-lined lakes.

Stay: Ngorongoro crater-rim lodge · Meals: All

Day 4

Into the Serengeti

Cross the plains into the Serengeti, game viewing as you go, to your camp in the central Seronera.

Stay: Serengeti tented camp · Meals: All

Day 5

Serengeti game drives

A full day in big-cat country, with an optional dawn balloon over the plains.

Stay: Serengeti tented camp · Meals: All

Day 6

Fly to Zanzibar

A light-aircraft flight to the coast, then into historic Stone Town. The pace softens; the sea air begins.

Stay: Stone Town boutique hotel · Meals: Breakfast

Day 7

Stone Town & spices

A guided walk through the UNESCO old town and a spice-farm visit, with a Swahili lunch among the cloves and cinnamon.

Stay: Stone Town boutique hotel · Meals: Breakfast, lunch

Day 8

To the north coast

Transfer to a beachfront retreat on the powder-white sands of the north. The rest of the day is yours.

Stay: Beachfront resort · Meals: All

Day 9

Dhow sailing & ocean

Sail a traditional dhow to a sandbank for snorkelling and a barefoot picnic, home into the sunset.

Stay: Beachfront resort · Meals: All

Day 10

Fly home

A last morning by the sea, then transfer to Zanzibar airport for your onward flight.

Meals: Breakfast

Queen Elizabeth: A Woman's Guide

Tree-climbing lions, elephant herds and the wildlife-lined Kazinga Channel.

Home / Uganda / Queen Elizabeth

Uganda's most popular savanna park is a place of wonderful variety — open plains and crater lakes beneath the snow-capped Rwenzori, a channel teeming with hippo and elephant, and the famous lions that climb the fig trees of the wild Ishasha sector. It's the classic game-viewing heart of a Uganda safari.

Why visit Queen Elizabeth?

Straddling the equator between the Rwenzori Mountains and Lake Edward, Queen Elizabeth packs an extraordinary range of habitats — savanna, wetland, forest and crater lakes — into one park. The result is classic big-game safari alongside primates, nearly 600 bird species, and scenery that shifts beautifully from one drive to the next.

The Kazinga Channel

The natural channel linking Lakes Edward and George is the park's beating heart, and an afternoon boat cruise along it is unmissable. You drift past one of the highest concentrations of hippo in Africa, elephants drinking at the edge, buffalo wallowing, and a riot of birds — kingfishers, pelicans, fish eagles and storks — lining the banks.

Tree-climbing lions of Ishasha

In the remote, southern Ishasha sector, lions have taken to lounging in the branches of the great fig trees — a behaviour seen in only a handful of places on earth. Tracking down a pride draped lazily overhead is a safari highlight, and the road through Ishasha conveniently links the park to gorilla country in Bwindi.

Wildlife you'll see

  • Big game: elephant, buffalo, lion, leopard and Uganda kob in good numbers.
  • Primates: chimpanzees in the dramatic Kyambura Gorge, plus baboons and monkeys.
  • Water & birds: hippo, crocodile and around 600 bird species — among Africa's richest lists.

Best time to visit

Game viewing is good year-round, but the dry months — June–September and December–February — concentrate wildlife around the water and make the tracks easier. The green seasons are lush, scenic and quieter. See our best time to visit Uganda guide for the full picture.

Where to stay & how to get there

We favour lodges with views over the channel or the Kyambura escarpment, and tucked-away camps in wild Ishasha for those continuing to Bwindi. The park is reached by light aircraft from Entebbe or as a scenic drive on the classic Uganda loop. We arrange every transfer and the boat cruise.

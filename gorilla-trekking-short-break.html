Gorilla Trekking Short Break

4 days Bwindi · Lake Bunyonyi Private from $3,900 / person
Enquire about this trip
Home / Uganda / Gorilla Trekking Short Break

Short on time but set on gorillas? This focused four-day break flies you straight to the south-west for an unforgettable hour with a mountain gorilla family in Bwindi, then a restful day beside the terraced shores of Lake Bunyonyi.

The fastest, gentlest way to a life-list encounter — easily added to a Kenya or Tanzania safari, or savoured on its own.

Day by day

Your 4-day journey

Day 1

Fly to Bwindi

Arrive in Entebbe and connect by light aircraft to the Bwindi airstrip, with the terraced hills unrolling below. Settle into your forest-edge lodge and meet your guide.

Stay: Bwindi forest lodge · Meals: All from lunch

Day 2

Gorilla trekking

An early briefing, then into the ancient forest with trackers ahead. The moment you reach the family — a silverback at rest, mothers and tumbling youngsters — is one you'll never forget. The afternoon is yours to rest and reflect.

Stay: Bwindi forest lodge · Meals: All

Day 3

Lake Bunyonyi

A short drive to the "place of little birds" — a serene day of canoes, birdsong and lakeside calm, or an optional Batwa community visit.

Stay: Lake Bunyonyi lodge · Meals: All

Day 4

Fly to Entebbe & depart

A morning by the lake, then a light-aircraft flight back to Entebbe for your onward connection.

Meals: Breakfast

Women-Only Tanzania

8 days Tarangire · Ngorongoro · Serengeti Hosted group of 8–12 from $8,900 / person
Enquire about this trip
Home / Tanzania / Women-Only Tanzania

Our sisterhood departure through Tanzania's north — the elephants of Tarangire, the Ngorongoro Crater and the endless Serengeti — in an intimate group of 8–12 women, led by a female host throughout.

Vetted camps, thoughtful pacing and genuine connection — including time with the Maasai women whose cooperatives we support. Come solo and leave with friends.

Day by day

Your 8-day journey

Day 1

Arrive & gather in Arusha

Met at Kilimanjaro Airport and welcomed to a leafy lodge, where the group comes together over a welcome dinner with your host.

Stay: Arusha country lodge · Meals: Dinner

Day 2

Tarangire National Park

An easy first safari day among the elephants and baobabs, settling into the rhythm together.

Stay: Tarangire tented camp · Meals: All

Day 3

Highlands & Maasai women

Up to the Ngorongoro highlands, with a visit to a Maasai women's beadwork cooperative — a warm, honest exchange over chai.

Stay: Ngorongoro crater-rim lodge · Meals: All

Day 4

Ngorongoro Crater floor

A full day in the crater — lions, rhino, elephant and flamingo-pink lakes — with a celebratory picnic on the floor.

Stay: Ngorongoro crater-rim lodge · Meals: All

Day 5

Into the Serengeti

Cross the plains into the Serengeti, game viewing as you go, to your camp in the central Seronera.

Stay: Serengeti tented camp · Meals: All

Day 6

Serengeti game drives

A full day in big-cat country, with an optional dawn balloon and a champagne bush breakfast for those who wish.

Stay: Serengeti tented camp · Meals: All

Day 7

Serengeti & sundowners

More unhurried drives, then a final golden-hour gathering with sundowners as the plains turn to amber.

Stay: Serengeti tented camp · Meals: All

Day 8

Fly home together

A last sunrise drive, then a light-aircraft flight back to Arusha — new friendships and a fuller heart for the journey home.

Meals: Breakfast
